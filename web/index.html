RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Know the boundaries.

This version runs one camera at a time. Camera count means configured channels, not verified online cameras.

Drawing a zone changes the next monitoring session. Stop and restart monitoring to apply it.

Intrusion and loitering use explicit rules. Falls, fights and general unusual behavior are not supported.

Live accuracy and capacity across 25 cameras remain unvalidated. Alerts require human review.
